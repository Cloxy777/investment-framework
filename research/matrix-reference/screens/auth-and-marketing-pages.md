## `/how-to-enter/` — "Як увійти на сайт, якщо ви в клубі" (How to sign in if you are in the club)
Static help. Club members get Matrix access at no extra cost. Steps: click "Увійти" at top (or button below) -> enter the email used to pay for the club -> "Надіслати код" -> a **6-digit code** arrives by email, valid **10 minutes** -> site remembers the device for **30 days**. Troubleshooting: check Spam/Promotions; wait 2–3 min and press "Надіслати код ще раз"; **max 5 codes per hour per address**. If the site can't see your payment: usually another email — use section "Платили за клуб з іншої пошти": enter payment email, press "Надіслати код на пошту оплати", confirm with emailed code. If paid today: appears next morning automatically; else use "Повідомити про помилку". Button "Перейти до входу" -> `/access/`.

## `/welcome/` — invitation landing / marketing ("Матриця Тараса Гука · Отримати доступ · Закрите тестування")
Hero: "One card instead of a hundred numbers" — daily computes hundreds of companies by one methodology; the card states the decision in words with the reason. CTAs "Отримати доступ" (get access, waiting-list/invite) and "Подивитись картку" (see a card). Stat tiles: **500+** companies today (thousands later) · **145** reporting metrics per company · **~200** computations per company daily · **600+** thresholds and rules. Section "A score from nowhere is worth nothing": explains eight quality components, separate price and risk view, every deduction named, gaps stated rather than guessed. States the fixed sequence per card: 145 SEC-filing metrics, 8 quality components, 10 signs, **34 red-flag checks**, 5 price lenses, 3 return scenarios; thresholds live in configuration (600+). ## `/no-access/` — "У вас немає доступу" (You have no access)
Shown when a visitor has no invitation: the site is in closed testing, cards open only to invitees ("not an error"). Ways in: (1) club member -> button "Я в клубі: увійти поштою"; (2) join the waiting list on the invitation page (invitations sent in batches, in sign-up order); (3) already invited -> open the link from the email or enter the code on the sign-in page; (4) lost/used code -> "Повідомити про помилку" for a new one. Buttons: "Я в клубі: увійти поштою", "Записатись у список очікування", "Ввести код запрошення". A contact mailbox is shown for requests. Note: closed testing is free; no payment data requested, no automatic charges.
