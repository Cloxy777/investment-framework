## B. Where this repo is stronger (not one-directional)

1. **Exit and trim machinery** (rows 25, 26): Phase 05/06, fair-value buy/sell/stop, 1.5% risk sizing, R/R ≥ 2:1, position cap — the Matrix has none of it.
2. **Rate-environment awareness** (row 19): a real gate and regime modifier; Matrix uses a fixed 4% yield constant.
3. **Cash-quality tests aimed at fraud** (row 10): FCF/NI conversion and its rolling-window disqualifier, the direct response to Valeant/Wirecard.
4. **Rule 0 discipline** (row 13): any missing input hard-stops; Matrix substitutes neutral values.
5. **Evidence-per-signal moat** (row 7): each signal needs a cited source.
6. **Structural safeguards against ourselves**: graveyard audit, benchmark vs MSCI Quality/QMJ, investor-philosophy check, override log, decision log, stale-score mechanism.
7. **Global universe and Lynch/Klarman-style downside underwriting** (Rule 7 bull/base/bear with 25% bear weight, `fair-value-methodology.md:237-247`).
8. **Execution safety**: safe-guard margin check, IBKR sync, healthcheck (no Matrix analogue).
9. **Scripted, tested maths** (`scripts/scoring/`, `scripts/tests/`): a re-implementable engine with hard-failing inputs.

## C. Where the Matrix is stronger

1. **Forward information** in growth (row 5) and **consensus-anchored expected return** (row 20).
2. **SBC-aware cash yield** (row 9, 14) — a real hole here.
3. **Cheap, deterministic flags** on numbers (row 21), and a visible "known risks" block.
4. **Change control at the data layer**: nightly snapshots, change feed, cause attribution (row 29).
5. **Explainability**: numeric "what must change" text (row 30).
6. **Anti-flip-flop mechanics**: caps and hysteresis (row 12).
7. **Portfolio aggregates with coverage disclosure** (row 35).
8. **Pre-registered kill-lines before earnings** (row 28).

## D. Caveats that limit every "adopt/adapt" above

- **Matrix constants are a 2026 regime** (fixed 4% good yield, index P/E 20.1, multiple 16 pessimistic, mega-cap ≥ $200B/≤20× tweak, index EPS growth fallback 13%). Never copy them as numbers (`RS:83,150`).
- **Matrix is one author's methodology in closed beta**; several rules are "not live" (`RM:90`), many thresholds are hidden ("600+ settings", OQ#12), and there are internal contradictions (`01-verification.md` C3, C4, E1, E2). No proposal below depends on the Low-confidence blocks (Capital & SBC table, STRONG BUY conditions, flag thresholds).
- **Double counting** (the Matrix's own "one fact, one penalty" rule, `RM:52`): several Matrix items overlap existing repo terms — net margin ↔ Profitability; owner earnings ↔ SBC; vs-index lens ↔ Rate Gate; own-history lens ↔ Forward PE range. Each proposal states its interaction explicitly.

New terms (not yet in glossary.md):
- **Stop-list** — a written list of business types the investor will not analyse for core holdings (e.g. banks, airlines).
- **Competence circle** — the set of business types the investor understands well enough to forecast.
- **Hysteresis / three-state gate** — pass at ≥80.0, "amber" between 78.0 and 80.0 for names already held, fail below 78.0 — prevents flip-flopping on tiny score changes.
- **Cycle peak** — top of a business cycle; earnings look unusually high, so the stock looks cheap.
- **Kill-line** — a pre-written threshold set *before* an earnings report; crossing it forces a specific action (rescore, trim review) with no debate afterwards.
- **Coverage (portfolio metrics)** — the share of the portfolio's weight for which a metric was actually available.
- **Cluster** — a group of holdings that share one main risk thesis (e.g. subscription software exposed to AI disruption).
- **Basket** — Matrix term for a business-type category in its competence registry.
