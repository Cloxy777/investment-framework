## 3. Data Gaps / Flags

1. **⚠️ MAJOR — Market Cap / share-count methodology correction (materially changes the score vs. 07-05).** The 2026-07-05 session (and 06-20/06-07 before it) computed Market Cap using `yfinance`'s `sharesOutstanding` field (1,659,709,932). Investigating why that figure understates Visa's actual market cap by ~13% this session: Visa has **five common-share classes** (A, B-1, B-2, B-3, C) with **different, time-varying, litigation-escrow-linked conversion ratios** into Class A-equivalents (confirmed from the Q3 FY2026 release's own disclosure: Class A diluted EPS $2.97 vs. Class B-1 $4.59 vs. Class C $11.86 for the *same* quarter — proof the classes are not 1:1 economically equivalent). `yfinance`'s `sharesOutstanding` field reflects **Class A shares only** (1,702M per the June 30, 2026 balance sheet, closer to but still not matching 1.660B — likely a slightly older Class A count). `yfinance`'s own `marketCap` field ($697,160,237,056, live-price-consistent as of the 07-28 close) implies a **much larger total share count of ~1,902M** — independently cross-checked two ways: (a) it is within 0.2% of Visa's own officially-filed Q3 FY2026 diluted weighted-average Class A-equivalent share count (1,898M, disclosed in the earnings release), and (b) three independent third-party data vendors (stockanalysis.com, macrotrends.net, companiesmarketcap.com, via live web search) all report Visa's current total shares outstanding as **~1.88–1.90 billion**, not ~1.66 billion. **This session adopts ~1,901.7M total diluted shares (yfinance's live-price-implied count, cross-validated against Visa's own filing and three independent vendors) — a correction to the prior three V sessions' Market Cap, EV, EV/EBIT, and FCF Yield inputs**, which were understated/overstated in the direction that made V look cheaper than it actually was. **Sensitivity check, shown transparently:** re-running this session's raw weighted valuation score with the *old* (uncorrected) 1.660B share count instead gives raw+Rate-Modifier ≈ **57.1** (vs. this session's corrected 64.6) — meaning of the ~10.0-point rise in the final valuation score since 07-05 (44.5 → 54.5), roughly **7.5 points is the methodology correction** and roughly **2.5 points is genuine drift** (elevated Q3 one-off opex modestly compressing TTM EBIT — see flag 3 below). **Not retroactively rewriting the 06-07/06-20/07-05 sessions** ## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Beta** | A stock's sensitivity to overall market moves. |
| **CAGR** | Compound Annual Growth Rate. |
| **CapEx** | Capital Expenditure. |
| **Catalyst window** | The timeframe (Rule 10, typically 18–24 months) within which a documented event is expected to close the price/fair-value gap. |
| **Composite Score** | This framework's blended 0.0–100.0 ranking combining Quality and Valuation Scores 50/50 — computed only for companies clearing the 80.0+ Quality Score gate. V: 34.5 this session. |
| **D&A** | Depreciation & Amortization. |
| **Debt Gate** | This framework's balance-sheet check on Net Debt/EBITDA (Hybrid Upgrade 5) — standard threshold <2.5×, relaxed to <4–6× for asset-light payment networks/exchanges with strong interest coverage and investment-grade ratings. Applied to V (a payment network) this session. |
| **Diluted weighted-average shares** | The average number of shares (including the dilutive effect of convertible securities/equity awards) used as the denominator for diluted EPS over a reporting period — for a multi-class company like Visa, each share class has its own diluted weighted-average count and its own EPS, since the classes are not 1:1 economically equivalent (see §3 flag 1). |
| **EBIT / EBITDA** | Operating profit before interest and taxes / before interest, taxes, D&A. |
| **EPS** | Earnings Per Share. |
| **EV / EV/EBIT** | Enterprise Value (market cap + net debt) / EV divided by EBIT. |
| **EY (Earnings Yield)** | 1 ÷ Forward PE, compared against the 10-Year Treasury yield. |
| **Fast Grower** | Lynch's term for >15%/yr EPS growth for 3+ years on a clean earnings base — this framework's PEG-eligibility trigger; V does not qualify this session (unchanged from 07-05). |
| **FCF / FCF Yield / FCF/NI conversion ratio** | Free Cash Flow; FCF ÷ Market Cap; FCF ÷ Net Income (checks accounting-profit quality). |
| **Forward PE** | Price ÷ next-twelve-months expected EPS. |
| **FV / PW Fair Value** | Fair Value / Probability-Weighted Fair Value (25% bull + 50% base + 25% bear). |
| **GAAP / Non-GAAP** | GAAP (Generally Accepted Accounting Principles) figures follow standardized US accounting rules; Non-GAAP figures are a company's own adjusted presentation that strips out items management deems non-recurring (e.g. severance, litigation provisions, intangible amortization) — this framework scores off GAAP figures throughout, consistent with not scoring self-reported adjustments (see §3 flag 3). |
| **Hard disqualifier** | A Quality Score condition that fails a company regardless of weighted score. |
| **Hurdle rate** | The minimum acceptable annual return (10% in this framework). |
| **Interchange fee (swipe fee) / MDL (multidistrict litigation)** | The fee an acquiring/merchant bank pays an issuing bank on a card transaction, set largely by the card network's rules; MDL is the consolidated federal litigation over these fees — Visa recorded a further $237M provision against this case in Q3 FY2026, part of this session's bear-case rationale. |
| **Invested Capital** | The total capital (debt + equity) put to work in a business — the denominator of ROIC. |
| **Moat** | A durable competitive advantage protecting a business's profits. |
| **MoS (Margin of Safety)** | The discount to fair value demanded before buying. |
| **Net Debt/EBITDA** | Leverage ratio — years of cash profit needed to pay off all debt. |
| **NI (Net Income)** | Accounting profit after all expenses. |
| **Net Margin** | Net Income ÷ Revenue. |
| **NOPAT** | Net Operating Profit After Tax — EBIT × (1 − effective tax rate); used to compute ROIC. |
| **NTM** | Next Twelve Months. |
| **PE (Price-to-Earnings) ratio / PEG ratio** | Share price ÷ EPS; PE ÷ earnings growth rate. |
| **PT (Price Target)** | An analyst's forecast of future price. |
| **Quality Score** | This framework's 0.0–100.0 score (0.0 = lowest quality) grading profitability, margins, growth, balance sheet, moat, and FCF quality; 80.0+ required to reach Phase 02/Composite Score. V this session: **85.6 — passes the gate.** |
| **R/R (Risk/Reward ratio)** | Expected gain ÷ expected loss — minimum 2:1 to enter. |
| **Rate Environment Gate / Rate Regime Modifier** | The pre-check comparing Earnings Yield to the 10-Year Treasury, plus the ±10 additive adjustment for the current Treasury-yield band. |
| **ROIC** | Return on Invested Capital — NOPAT ÷ Invested Capital. |
| **Rule 0 / Rule 6 / Rule 9 / Rule 10** | This framework's standing instructions to always fetch a live price first; require a minimum 2:1 risk/reward before entering; force re-valuation on specific fundamental triggers; and separate intrinsic value from market price with a documented catalyst and timeline. |
| **Shareholder yield** | Dividend yield + net buyback yield combined. |
| **TTM (Trailing Twelve Months)** | The most recent 12 months of reported results. |
| **Upside/Downside Modifier (Expected-Return Modifier)** | Additive ±15 score adjustment based on expected annual return vs the 10% hurdle. |
| **VAS (Value-Added Services)** | Visa's non-core-network product line (fraud/risk tools, tokenization, advisory, issuing/acceptance support) — its fastest-growing revenue segment, now growing 34% YoY and cited as this session's Growth sub-score TAM-expansion bonus evidence. |
| **YTD (Year-to-Date)** | The cumulative change in price since the start of the calendar year. |
