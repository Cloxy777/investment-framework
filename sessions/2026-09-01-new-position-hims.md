## §9. Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **503A / 503B (compounding pharmacy classifications)** | Two distinct legal categories under the US Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act for pharmacies that "compound" (custom-mix) drugs rather than sell FDA-approved manufactured products: a **503A** pharmacy compounds patient-specific prescriptions under state pharmacy-board oversight; a **503B outsourcing facility** compounds at larger scale (often from bulk drug substances, without a patient-specific prescription in hand) under closer FDA oversight. The FDA's 2026 proposal to exclude semaglutide/tirzepatide/liraglutide from the "503B Bulks List" (§0/§7) would restrict 503B-scale bulk compounding of those drugs, not 503A patient-specific compounding — a distinction that matters for assessing HIMS's direct regulatory exposure. |
| **8-K** | A US public company's filing disclosing a material event (here, quarterly earnings, a receivables-facility agreement, and a credit-agreement amendment) to the SEC promptly after it occurs. |
| **AFS (Available-for-Sale) securities** | A balance-sheet classification for liquid debt securities held as a cash-management buffer; netted into cash for this framework's Net Debt calculation. |
| **CAGR** | Compound Annual Growth Rate. |
| **Deferred acquisition payable** | A fixed-dollar liability for consideration owed on a completed acquisition, not yet paid — $703.0M on HIMS's balance sheet, tied to the Eucalyptus Health acquisition, central to this framework's ongoing Net Debt/EBITDA judgment-call flag. |
| **FCF/NI conversion ratio** | Free Cash Flow ÷ Net Income — an earnings-quality check. |
| **FTC (Federal Trade Commission)** | The US federal agency enforcing consumer-protection and antitrust law. Sued HIMS (joined by California and Utah) on 2026-07-29/31 alleging deceptive health-data sharing with advertisers and deceptive subscription-billing/cancellation practices — a genuine, dated, material legal development flagged this session, though not yet a scored financial input (no disclosed reserve or settlement figure exists). |
| **Gross Margin** | Gross Profit ÷ Revenue. |
| **Hard disqualifier** | One of three Quality Score conditions that fails a company regardless of its weighted score; none independently fires for HIMS under the primary Net Debt/EBITDA reading (though one would under a conservative reading — see the 2026-08-11 session). |
| **Moat** | A durable competitive advantage. |
| **Net Debt/EBITDA** | Net debt ÷ EBITDA, this framework's primary balance-sheet-risk gate; 2.10x for HIMS on the primary reading, unchanged since 08-11. |
| **Net Margin / ROIC** | Net Income ÷ Revenue; Return on Invested Capital — both remain deeply negative for HIMS on a TTM basis. |
| **PCAC (Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee)** | An FDA advisory committee that convened 23–24 July 2026 to review peptides for the compounding bulk-substances list — voted to recommend 6 peptides, none of them semaglutide or tirzepatide, per the 2026-08-11 session; unchanged this session. |
