| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Conglomerate rule** | This framework's instruction to consolidate a captive financial subsidiary's debt (and capital spending) into the relevant ratios rather than carving it out favorably — the central driver of Volkswagen's Balance Sheet and FCF Quality findings, same treatment as Toyota (TM) and Stellantis (STLA). |
| **Hard disqualifier** | A Quality Score condition that fails a company regardless of weighted score; two independently fire for Volkswagen (Net Debt/EBITDA; FCF/NI conversion). |

| **Net Debt/EBITDA** | This framework's primary balance-sheet-risk gate; Volkswagen's is 8.74×, more than double both the standard 2.5× and asset-light-override 4× thresholds. |
| **Preference share (Vorzugsaktie)** | Volkswagen's non-voting, higher-dividend share class (VOW3) — the ticker used as the primary instrument in this session, distinct from the voting ordinary shares (VOW). |
| **Quality Score** | This framework's 0.0–100.0 continuous quality grade; 80.0+ required to proceed to valuation scoring. Volkswagen scored 6.1. |
| **ROIC** | Return on Invested Capital; Volkswagen's is 2.16% (TTM), depressed by its captive-finance segment's leverage structure. |
