## Glossary

| **Active power capacity / Contracted power capacity** | Active power capacity is the electrical power already energized and in live production use; contracted power capacity is the larger forward total secured but not yet fully online. CRWV: 1.5 GW active, ~3.7 GW contracted as of Q2 2026. |
| **Backlog** | The dollar value of signed customer orders not yet recognized as revenue. CRWV's revenue backlog was ~$104B as of 2026-06-30. |
| **CAGR** | Compound Annual Growth Rate — the smoothed yearly growth rate that gets you from a start value to an end value over several years. |
