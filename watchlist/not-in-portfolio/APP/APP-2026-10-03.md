## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AXON / AXON 2.0** | AppLovin's proprietary AI ad-auction engine — the Network Effect moat-signal mechanism cited for APP (more auctions → better predictions → higher advertiser ROAS → more budget → more auctions). |
| **Composite Score** | This framework's blended Quality + Valuation ranking number (50/50), computed only for companies clearing the 80.0+ Quality Score gate. APP: 29.1 (nominally a "BUY, Full 6–8%" band). |
| **MAX (AppLovin)** | AppLovin's ad-mediation platform, embedded via SDK by mobile publishers — the Switching Costs moat-signal evidence cited for APP. |
| **MoS (Margin of Safety)** | How far below fair value the buy price is set, as a cushion against being wrong. Part of why this score band's Risk/Reward geometry fails for APP despite the Composite Score landing in a BUY band. |
| **PEG ratio** | PE ÷ earnings growth rate. Not applied for APP — no reliable earnings base post the 2025 mobile-apps divestiture. |
| **PW (Probability-Weighted) Fair Value** | This framework's blended bull/base/bear fair value estimate (25%/50%/25%). APP: $304.35, ~7% below the $328.88 live price and well below the $501.94 Street mean consensus target. |
| **Quality Score** | This framework's 0.0–100.0 continuous quality grade; a company must score 80.0+ to proceed to Phase 02. APP: 96.7. |
| **R/R (Risk/Reward ratio)** | (Expected gain) ÷ (Expected loss) on a trade — this framework requires at least 2:1 before entering. APP's primary basis computes 1.00:1, which is why no order is placed despite the Composite Score. |
| **ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)** | An advertiser's revenue-per-ad-dollar-spent metric AppLovin's AXON engine optimizes for — cited as the mechanism behind AppLovin's growth, not as pricing-power evidence (Brand Premium moat signal marked not demonstrated). |
| **SDK (Software Development Kit)** | A packaged integration a publisher embeds in its app — AppLovin's MAX SDK is the specific lock-in mechanism behind the Switching Costs moat signal. |
| **TTM (Trailing Twelve Months)** | The most recent four reported quarters combined — the basis for APP's Net Margin, ROIC, and gross-margin inputs. |
| **Valuation Score** | This framework's 0.0–100.0 continuous score (0.0 = cheapest, 100.0 = most expensive). APP: 54.9. |
| **TRO (Temporary Restraining Order)** | A short-term emergency court order issued before a full hearing; AppLovin's TRO request against Unity was denied on 2026-10-01 (denial does not decide the underlying case). |
| **Pixel (AppLovin Axon Ads Pixel)** | A tracking snippet merchants install so AXON can see which ads lead to purchases; its install count/traffic is an early gauge of AppLovin's e-commerce expansion. |
| **Rate Regime Modifier** | Additive valuation-score adjustment from the 10Y Treasury bracket; now +10 for APP because the 10Y is above 5% (5.24%). |
