## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)** | The annualized run-rate value of a subscription business's contracted recurring revenue at a point in time; CrowdStrike disclosed $5.84B ARR as of 31 Jul 2026, +25% YoY. |
| **CAGR** | Compound Annual Growth Rate — the smoothed yearly growth rate that gets you from a start value to an end value over several years. |
| **EBIT / EBITDA** | Earnings Before Interest and Taxes / before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization. |
| **FCF (Free Cash Flow)** | Cash generated by the business after capital expenditure. |
| **FCF/NI conversion ratio** | Free Cash Flow ÷ Net Income, an earnings-quality check; a low or undefined ratio without a documented CapEx explanation is a red flag. CrowdStrike's FY2025/FY2026 ratio is undefined/deeply negative (both years had negative Net Income), firing this framework's hard disqualifier. |
| **Gartner Magic Quadrant** | A widely-cited analyst-firm ranking of vendors along "completeness of vision" and "ability to execute"; CrowdStrike is a 7-time consecutive Leader in the Endpoint Protection Magic Quadrant. |
| **Hard disqualifier** | One of three Quality Score conditions that fails a company regardless of its weighted score; CrowdStrike's FCF/NI conversion ratio fires this one independently of the 71.6 weighted score. |
| **Net-new ARR** | The dollar increase in ARR added during a period; CrowdStrike added a record $332.8M in Q2 FY2027. |
| **Net Debt/EBITDA** | Net debt (total debt minus cash) divided by EBITDA — a leverage ratio; deeply negative for CrowdStrike (net cash position). |
| **Net Retention Rate (NRR / Dollar-Based Net Retention)** | The percentage of recurring revenue retained and expanded from an existing customer cohort over 12 months. |
| **Quality Score** | This framework's 0–100.0 continuous quality grade; a company must score 80.0+ to proceed to Phase 02. CrowdStrike scores 71.6. |
| **Rolling window (hard disqualifier)** | This framework's 2026-08-05 clarification: the "N+ consecutive years" hard-disqualifier tests are re-evaluated fresh each session against the most recently completed fiscal years, not treated as a permanent scar — but a single strong quarter or partial year cannot clear a disqualifier; the entire most-recently-completed fiscal year must be genuinely positive. |
| **ROIC** | Return on Invested Capital. |
| **Rule 0** | This framework's standing instruction to always fetch a live, current price before any valuation work. |
| **Rule 9** | This framework's standing list of fundamental-event triggers (earnings, guidance revision, M&A, management change, macro shift, >15% unexplained move) that mandate re-valuation. |
| **SBC (Stock-Based Compensation)** | Employee pay in company shares/options; a non-cash expense that inflates FCF relative to GAAP net income but remains a real, dilutive economic cost. |
| **Stock split** | A corporate action that increases share count by a fixed ratio while proportionally reducing price per share; CrowdStrike effected a 4-for-1 split effective 1 Jul 2026 — all figures here are post-split. |
| **TTM (Trailing Twelve Months)** | The most recent 12 months of reported financial results. |
