## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **CAGR** | Compound Annual Growth Rate — the smoothed yearly growth rate that gets you from a start value to an end value over several years. |
| **FCF** | Free Cash Flow — cash a business generates after running and maintaining itself, available to return to shareholders or reinvest. |
| **FCF/NI conversion ratio** | Free Cash Flow ÷ Net Income — checks whether reported accounting profit is actually turning into real cash. |
| **Hard disqualifier** | One of three Quality Score conditions that fails a company regardless of its weighted sub-score total. |
| **Moat** | Warren Buffett's term for a durable competitive advantage that protects a business's profits from competitors. |
| **Moat Signal** | This framework's 5-point Quality Score checklist scoring the "Moat" concept, each signal markable TRUE only against a cited source. |
| **Net Debt/EBITDA** | Net debt (total debt minus cash) divided by EBITDA — a leverage ratio; this framework's primary balance-sheet-risk gate. |
| **Owner Earnings** | Warren Buffett's adjusted cash-flow measure: Net Income + D&A − *Maintenance* CapEx only (excludes growth CapEx) — used here because Recordati's growth CapEx (in-licensing deals) exceeds 30% of total CapEx every year. |
| **Quality Score** | This framework's 0.0–100.0 grade blending profitability, margins, growth, balance sheet, moat, and FCF quality; a company must score ≥80.0 to reach Phase 02 valuation scoring at all. REC.MI scores 63.3. |
| **ROIC** | Return on Invested Capital — how efficiently a company turns invested capital into profit. |
| **TAM** | Total Addressable Market — the total revenue opportunity available if a company captured 100% of its target market. |
