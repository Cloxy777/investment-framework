## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **bps (basis points)** | 1 bps = 0.01 percentage points. |
| **Comparable sales (comps)** | Sales growth at stores open at least a year, stripping out new-store growth — LULU's Q2 FY2026 comps fell 9%. |
| **FCF/NI conversion ratio** | Free Cash Flow ÷ Net Income — checks whether reported accounting profit is actually turning into real cash. |
| **Form 4** | A SEC filing disclosing an insider's (officer/director/10%+ owner's) purchase or sale of company stock. |
| **Quality Score** | A 0–100.0 grade blending profitability, margins, growth, balance sheet, moat, and FCF quality; a company must score ≥80.0 to be eligible for Phase 02 valuation scoring. LULU scores 62.2. |
| **Rule 9** | This framework's list of fundamental events that force an immediate re-valuation regardless of schedule: quarterly earnings, a guidance revision, a management change, material M&A, a macro shift, or a >15% stock-price move with no identified cause. |
| **SG&A (Selling, General & Administrative expenses)** | Marketing/selling costs plus corporate overhead — everything between Gross Profit and Operating Income that isn't cost of goods sold. |
| **Turnaround Sub-Gate** | The conditional path (Hybrid Upgrade 4) letting a company failing 2-4 quality criteria still enter as a small position if it passes 5 specific tests. Not independently re-run this session — LULU's Quality Score isn't close enough to 80.0 for it to matter. |
| **YoY (Year-over-Year)** | A growth-rate comparison against the same period one year earlier. |
