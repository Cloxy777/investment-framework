## Glossary

See [framework/glossary.md](../../../framework/glossary.md) for the standing definitions file. Terms used above:

- **GDS (Global Distribution System)** — a B2B travel-technology platform (Amadeus, Sabre, Travelport) aggregating airline/hotel/car-rental inventory into a single bookable marketplace for travel agencies.
- **NDC (New Distribution Capability)** — an IATA data standard letting airlines sell direct/via-agent without routing through a traditional GDS — a documented long-term disintermediation risk to GDS operators.
- **Hard disqualifier** — one of three Quality Score conditions that fails a company regardless of weighted score; none fire for AMS.MC.
- **Net Debt/EBITDA** — a leverage ratio; this framework's primary balance-sheet-risk gate.
- **Moat Signal** — this framework's 5-point Quality Score checklist scoring a durable competitive advantage against cited evidence.
- **Quality Score** — this framework's 0-100.0 continuous quality grade; a company must score 80.0+ to proceed to Phase 02. AMS.MC scores 70.5 on its first-ever computation.
- **ROIC** — Return on Invested Capital.
- **CAGR** — Compound Annual Growth Rate.
- **TAM (Total Addressable Market)** — the total revenue opportunity available if a company captured its entire target market.
